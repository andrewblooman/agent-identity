How the score works

  • Each area starts at 100. A critical finding takes off 45, high 22, medium 8 and low 3.
  • An agent's score is a weighted average: data 30%, identity 25%, secrets 15%, network 15%, runtime 10%, governance 5%. Areas with nothing assessed are left out.
  • A critical finding limits the agent to 40, and a high finding limits it to 70, so good results elsewhere can't hide them.
  • The overall score averages the mean of all agents with the mean of the worst quarter.
  • Scores below 75% check coverage are marked provisional, because checks that couldn't run don't count against them.
  • Use the score to decide what to look at first. It isn't a measure of how likely a breach is.
